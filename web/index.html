G-Nome engine (work in progress)

A reimplementation of 7th Level's Runtime Player that runs G-Nome (1997) from its own scripts, in your browser. You need your own copy of the game: nothing here contains game data, and your files are read on your computer and never uploaded.

Load your copy of G-Nome

  • Pick the game CD, its G-NOME folder (the one with G-NOME.BIN), or a .zip of either. You can also drop it here.
  • It loads about 75 MB into memory, so give it a few seconds.
  • Work in progress: the intro, menus, options and briefings work; missions show the battlefield but units don't fight yet.
